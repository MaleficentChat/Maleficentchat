Meet people on Maleficent Chat

Maleficent Chat is an online chat community where you can join conversations, discover people with shared interests and keep in touch with friends. Start by creating an account or signing in, then explore the community at your own pace.

Chat rooms Friends & messages Community features

Chat rooms and live conversations

Join the main room or explore available rooms to talk with other members. Room descriptions and room rules help you choose a conversation that suits you.

Friends and private messages

Build your friends list, manage requests and continue one-to-one conversations with private messages. Profile tools help members discover one another.

More ways to participate

Share community posts, read announcements, play mini-games and use chat tools such as GIFs and doodles where available. Reporting and moderation features support respectful participation.

Be respectful, protect your personal information and report content that concerns you. Never share passwords, verification codes or sensitive details in a public chat.
